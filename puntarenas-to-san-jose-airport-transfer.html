Puntarenas to San José Airport Transfer

Private transfer from the Puntarenas cruise pier to Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) — for passengers disembarking and flying home or onward from San José.

When your cruise ends in Puntarenas and your flight departs from San José (SJO), a private transfer offers direct pier-to-airport service without shared shuttle delays. Driving time is typically around 90 minutes depending on traffic and route — allow generous buffer before international flights.

Tour details are provided on confirmation. Always check your cruise ship arrival and departure times before booking.

Why cruise passengers choose this transfer

  • Direct private vehicle — no shared shuttle wait
  • Flexibility on pickup time coordinated with disembarkation
  • Luggage space for full cruise bags
  • Professional driver familiar with pier and airport routes
  • Can often accommodate stop requests — confirm in advance

Good to know before booking

  • Book based on your flight time — allow 3+ hours before international departure
  • Traffic on routes toward San José can vary significantly
  • Provide ship name, disembarkation date and passenger count
  • Reverse route (SJO to Puntarenas pier) may be available — inquire when booking
  • This is transport, not a sightseeing tour — see private tours for excursions

What to bring

  • Passport and flight confirmation
  • USD or colones for tips if desired
  • Phone with booking confirmation and driver contact
  • Snacks for the drive if needed

Who this transfer is best for

Cruise passengers ending their voyage in Puntarenas with flights from Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO). Families with luggage, groups wanting predictable timing, and anyone avoiding uncertain shared shuttle schedules.

Who should choose a different option

Passengers with a full day before a late flight might combine a shore excursion with separate transfer arrangements. Those staying in Costa Rica should book onward transport to their hotel, not the airport.
