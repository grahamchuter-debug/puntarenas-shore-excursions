One Day in Puntarenas

Sample itineraries for cruise passengers with a single day ashore — focused on Costa Rica nature, wildlife and realistic port timing.

Most cruise ships spend one day in Puntarenas. The port itself is compact; the memorable experiences lie in the surrounding Central Pacific region. Below are three practical approaches — always align with your ship's schedule. Tour details are provided on confirmation.

Itinerary A: First-time Costa Rica (full day)

Best tour: Best of Costa Rica Tour (approx. 7 hours)

  • Disembark early and meet your excursion near the pier
  • Scenic drive through mango, coffee and citrus countryside
  • Wildlife stops, cultural highlights and included local lunch
  • Return with buffer time before all-aboard

Ideal if you want a balanced introduction without committing to one activity like horseback riding or zip lining.

Itinerary B: Wildlife & crocodile focus

Best tours: Tarcoles River Crocodile Safari or Rainforest Wildlife Tour

  • Boat safari on the Tarcoles River — famous for large American crocodiles
  • Monkey, bird and iguana viewing along the riverbanks
  • Rainforest option adds canopy bridges or adventure park time
  • Optional chocolate or coffee tasting on select departures

Choose the crocodile-focused safari for maximum wildlife; choose the rainforest combo for canopy plus river.

Itinerary C: Active adventure day

Best tour: Eco Horseback & Crocodile River Safari

  • Morning rainforest trail ride through Vista Los Suenos area
  • Afternoon river boat safari for crocodiles and tropical birds
  • Moderate activity level — reasonable fitness recommended

Not ideal for passengers with significant mobility limitations or those who prefer minimal walking.

Itinerary D: Private or half-day

Options: Private Charming Puntarenas, Private Highlights, or Private Half Day

  • Custom pacing for families, multi-generational groups or photographers
  • Half-day private tours leave afternoon free near the waterfront
  • Combine wildlife with town time if your group prefers flexibility

Timing checklist

  • Note official arrival and all-aboard times from your cruise line
  • Book excursions that guarantee return before ship departure
  • Allow 30–60 minutes after tour end for pier re-entry
  • Keep ship contact number and port agent details handy
