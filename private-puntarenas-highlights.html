Private Puntarenas Highlights

Full-day private Puntarenas shore excursion with professional naturalist guide and private air-conditioned van — Central Pacific wildlife and landscapes at your group's pace.

Private Puntarenas Highlights delivers the most comprehensive private sightseeing option for cruise passengers who want expert interpretation of Costa Rica's Central Pacific region. A professional naturalist guide and dedicated van take your group through wildlife viewing areas, scenic countryside and key regional highlights with flexibility for photo stops and rest breaks.

Tour details are provided on confirmation. Always check your cruise ship arrival and departure times before booking.

Why cruise passengers choose this tour

  • Professional naturalist guide — deeper wildlife interpretation
  • Private air-conditioned van for your party only
  • Full-day coverage of Central Pacific highlights
  • Flexible photo and rest stops
  • Premium private alternative to shared Best of Costa Rica

Good to know before booking

  • Higher per-person cost for small groups — better value at 4+ passengers
  • Snack included; full lunch may be at additional cost depending on operator
  • Discuss wildlife priorities (crocodiles, monkeys, scenery) when inquiring
  • Compare with Private Charming Puntarenas for river-focused private day with lunch
  • Shorter port call? Consider half-day private

What to bring

  • Binoculars and camera with zoom
  • Sturdy walking shoes
  • Sun protection and insect repellent
  • Rain jacket and personal water
  • Ship card and confirmation details

Who this tour is best for

Discerning travelers, wildlife enthusiasts and multi-generational families who want a private naturalist-led day without sharing a coach. Photographers and birders especially benefit from flexible stops.

Who should choose a different tour

Budget travelers and solo passengers may prefer shared tours. Passengers wanting a fully self-designed itinerary should book Private Vehicle & Guide Half Day. For river-centric private day with lunch, choose Private Charming Puntarenas.
