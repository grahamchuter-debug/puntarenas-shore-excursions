Tarcoles River Crocodile Safari

The signature Puntarenas wildlife tour — eco-boat safari on the Tarcoles River to observe American crocodiles, tropical birds and rainforest river life up close.

The Tarcoles River is famous among naturalists for its dense population of American crocodiles — some exceeding four metres in length. This Puntarenas shore excursion combines a guided boat safari with scenic drives through the Central Pacific countryside, often including stops for monkeys, iguanas and bird viewing. It is the go-to choice for passengers whose priority is Costa Rica wildlife.

Tour details are provided on confirmation. Always check your cruise ship arrival and departure times before booking.

Why cruise passengers choose this tour

  • Highest concentration of crocodile viewing near Puntarenas port
  • Easy activity level — seated boat safari
  • Expert naturalist commentary on river ecosystem
  • Often combined with countryside or adventure park stops
  • Ideal Puntarenas wildlife tour when you do not want horseback or zip lines

Good to know before booking

  • Wildlife is wild — sightings vary by season and conditions
  • Keep hands inside the boat; follow guide safety instructions
  • Some departures add chocolate or coffee tasting — check itinerary
  • Shared tour format unless you book a private option
  • For canopy plus river, see rainforest wildlife tour

What to bring

  • Binoculars if you have them
  • Camera with zoom lens for crocodile photography
  • Sun hat, sunscreen and insect repellent
  • Light rain jacket
  • Closed-toe shoes for boarding

Who this tour is best for

Passengers who want maximum wildlife impact with minimal physical demand — including older travelers, photographers and anyone specifically interested in the Puntarenas crocodile river safari experience.

Who should choose a different tour

Choose the Best of Costa Rica for a broader cultural overview with lunch, or the horseback combo if you want more physical adventure. Private groups may prefer Private Charming Puntarenas.
