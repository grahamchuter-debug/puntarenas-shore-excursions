Eco Horseback & Crocodile River Safari

Active Puntarenas shore excursion combining rainforest trail riding with a Tarcoles River boat safari — two iconic Central Pacific experiences in one cruise day.

This Puntarenas cruise excursion pairs guided horseback riding through rainforest trails near the Vista Los Suenos area with an eco-boat safari on the Tarcoles River — home to enormous American crocodiles and abundant tropical bird life. It is one of the most action-packed nature days available from the port without zip lining.

Tour details are provided on confirmation. Always check your cruise ship arrival and departure times before booking.

Why cruise passengers choose this tour

  • Two distinct experiences — trail ride plus river safari — in one booking
  • Strong wildlife focus on the Tarcoles River crocodile habitat
  • Rainforest immersion from horseback rather than a coach window
  • More adventurous than the Best of Costa Rica overview tour
  • Popular with passengers who have already cruised Costa Rica before

Good to know before booking

  • Weight and health restrictions may apply for horseback riding — confirm on booking
  • Long pants recommended for riding; boat portion is relaxed
  • Shared tour with other cruise passengers
  • Not suitable for passengers uncomfortable around horses or open water
  • See also crocodile-only safari for easier pacing

What to bring

  • Long pants and closed-toe shoes
  • Insect repellent and sunscreen
  • Change of shirt if desired after riding
  • Camera with strap — keep hands free on horseback
  • Rain layer and personal water bottle

Who this tour is best for

Fit, adventurous cruise passengers who want an active Costa Rica day combining horseback riding and crocodile river viewing. Good for couples and groups comfortable with moderate physical activity.

Who should choose a different tour

Passengers with mobility limitations, back issues, or discomfort on horses should choose the Tarcoles River safari, rainforest wildlife tour, or a private tour with adjusted pacing.
