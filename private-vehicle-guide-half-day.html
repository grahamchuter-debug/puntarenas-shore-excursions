Private Vehicle & Guide Half Day

Customizable private half-day Puntarenas shore excursion — your guide, your vehicle, your priorities for a flexible Costa Rica port day.

This is the most flexible private Puntarenas cruise excursion format: approximately four hours with a dedicated guide and private transport. You design the focus — wildlife viewing toward Tarcoles, scenic countryside drives, town exploration, photo stops or a abbreviated rainforest visit — within realistic driving distances from the pier.

Tour details are provided on confirmation. Always check your cruise ship arrival and departure times before booking.

Why cruise passengers choose this tour

  • Maximum flexibility — you set the priorities
  • Leaves afternoon free for Puntarenas waterfront or ship
  • Ideal for shorter port calls or late ship arrival
  • Private transport avoids shared coach schedules
  • Good for mixed groups with varying interests

Good to know before booking

  • Four hours limits how far you can travel — discuss realistic goals
  • Full crocodile safari + rainforest may not fit — prioritize one focus
  • Entrance fees for attractions may be extra depending on itinerary
  • Compare full-day options: Private Highlights, Private Charming Puntarenas
  • Share mobility needs and interests when requesting a quote

What to bring

  • Comfortable shoes appropriate to your chosen stops
  • Sun protection and water
  • Cash for optional entries, food or souvenirs
  • Ship card and port map
  • List of priorities to share with your guide

Who this tour is best for

Groups who want private Puntarenas tours without committing to a full seven-hour day — including passengers combining town time with a morning excursion, or ships with tighter port windows.

Who should choose a different tour

Passengers wanting a complete wildlife day should choose Tarcoles safari or rainforest wildlife tour. Those wanting a structured private full day with naturalist should pick Private Highlights.
