Private Charming Puntarenas

Private Puntarenas shore excursion with dedicated vehicle and guide — intimate exploration of the Tarcoles River ecosystem and Central Pacific charm.

Private Charming Puntarenas is designed for cruise passengers who prefer a personal experience over a shared coach. Your private guide and vehicle allow flexible pacing through the Central Pacific countryside, with emphasis on the diverse Tarcoles River ecosystem — crocodiles, birds and tropical forest margins — plus included lunch at a local stop.

Tour details are provided on confirmation. Always check your cruise ship arrival and departure times before booking.

Why cruise passengers choose this tour

  • Private vehicle — no waiting on large groups
  • Intimate wildlife focus on Tarcoles River habitat
  • Included lunch at a relaxed pace
  • Guide attention for questions and photo stops
  • Good private alternative to shared crocodile safari tours

Good to know before booking

  • Priced per vehicle/group — compare per-person cost for your party size
  • Itinerary can emphasize wildlife or scenic stops — discuss preferences when booking
  • Private tours should still respect all-aboard buffer times
  • Not fully custom like the half-day private vehicle option
  • More structured than Private Highlights full-day naturalist tour

What to bring

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Camera, binoculars and sun protection
  • Insect repellent and rain layer
  • Ship card and booking confirmation
  • Cash for optional souvenirs

Who this tour is best for

Couples, families and small groups who want a private Puntarenas wildlife experience without designing every detail themselves. Ideal when you value the Tarcoles River ecosystem and prefer lunch included in a relaxed private setting.

Who should choose a different tour

For maximum itinerary control, choose Private Vehicle & Guide Half Day. For a longer naturalist-led day, see Private Puntarenas Highlights. Budget-conscious solo travelers may prefer shared Best of Costa Rica.
