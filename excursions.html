Puntarenas Shore Excursions

Compare Puntarenas Costa Rica shore excursions for cruise passengers — wildlife, rainforest, private tours and transfers. Always check your cruise ship arrival and departure times before booking.

Puntarenas is a gateway port, not a destination in itself. The best excursions reach into Costa Rica's Central Pacific countryside for crocodile river safaris, rainforest experiences, coffee and chocolate tastings, and scenic drives toward the Jaco and Tarcoles region.
