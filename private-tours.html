Private tour options for cruise passengers who want a dedicated vehicle, flexible pacing and personalized Costa Rica experiences from the Puntarenas port.

Shared coach tours work well for many passengers, but private Puntarenas cruise excursions make sense when you travel with family, need accessibility adjustments, want photo stops on your schedule, or simply prefer not to wait on a large group. When to choose a private tour

  • Your group has mixed mobility — you control rest stops and walking pace
  • You want dedicated time for photography at wildlife viewing points
  • You're traveling with young children who need flexibility
  • Your ship has a shorter port call — consider the half-day option
  • You prefer a naturalist guide focused on your questions, not a bus microphone
